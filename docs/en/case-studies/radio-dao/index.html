Context

Radio Barrio Centenario is a community radio station that now broadcasts around the clock on its own digital platform: a mobile app for neighbors, a web player, and an Admin Console where the team manages shows, the weekly schedule, votes and announcements. I designed and built it end to end through Shape Creative Studio, my studio.

The challenge

A neighborhood radio runs on volunteers, a tight budget and an audience of all ages, including older adults. The challenge was to bring it to the phone without losing what makes it theirs: neighbors feeling that the station belongs to them. The app had to be as simple as turning on a radio, and the team needed to run the schedule without relying on a technician.

My approach

As simple as a radio

I started from who listens: neighbors of every age, many of them not used to apps. Large type, generous buttons and a main screen with one obvious action: listen.

Frictionless participation

The first version explored decentralized governance (DAO). In the live version, participation was simplified into three gestures any neighbor understands:

  • Reactions: "Like" songs and share your mood live.
  • Votes: neighborhood proposals with clear quorum and closing dates.
  • Announcements: neighborhood notices built into the menu, no extra screens.

One ecosystem, three pieces

I designed and built the whole platform, from the user experience to the infrastructure.

  • Mobile app (React Native + Expo): 24/7 streaming with background audio, native controls, reactions, votes and schedule.
  • Admin Console (Next.js): the team manages the weekly schedule, shows, music blocks, votes and the broadcast engine, and follows the audience in real time.
  • Host workflow: hosts upload their recorded shows from the web and they go into the schedule.
  • Web player: the station can also be heard in the browser at radiobarriocentenario.cl.
