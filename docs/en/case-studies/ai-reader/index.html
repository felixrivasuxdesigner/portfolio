Context

Chucao (formerly LectorIA) is a B2B platform for schools that assesses oral reading fluency. Children read stories on a tablet alongside Chu, a Chilean chucao bird, while AI measures speed, accuracy and word-by-word errors right on the device. Teachers review the results in a web dashboard. I designed and built it through Shape Creative Studio, my studio.

The challenge

Measuring reading fluency today takes a teacher with a printed text and a stopwatch, one child at a time. It is slow, it depends on who assesses, and reading in front of an adult who is timing you makes children anxious. The challenge was to automate the measurement without losing pedagogical rigor, make it feel like a game rather than a test, and protect minors' voices under Chile's new data protection law (Ley 21.719).

Chucao Design System

A warm language inspired by the Chilean forest: forest green for action, the terracotta of the chucao's breast as accent and cream anti-glare surfaces for fatigue-free reading. Literata for reading and Plus Jakarta Sans for the interface.

My approach

Two users, two experiences

The product has two sides with opposite needs:

  • Student: tablet, one task per screen, large text and no signs of being assessed.
  • Teacher: a high-density dashboard to review assessments in batches, listen to the audio and export reports.
  • School leadership: aggregated indicators by class and school for improvement plans.

Chu, the heart of the experience

I replaced the first version's generic owl with Chu, a chucao, a bird from the Chilean forest. Chu has 10 emotional states (greets, thinks, listens, celebrates, encourages after a mistake) inspired by Duolingo's positive reinforcement. There are no error sounds or visible timers: time is measured, but the child never sees it.

The solution

A dual platform with the AI running inside the tablet:

  • Edge AI: Whisper (whisper.rn) transcribes speech on the tablet itself with a ~31 MB quantized model; after the first download it works offline.
  • Word-by-word analysis: each word is classified as correct, omitted, substituted or inserted, and the app computes reading speed (WCPM) and the Ministry of Education level.
  • Teacher dashboard: batch review, a player that syncs the audio with the word breakdown at 0.75x, 1x or 1.25x, and PDF reports for parents.
  • Offline-first: assessments are stored on the tablet and synced when the connection returns, built for classrooms with unreliable internet.

Privacy by design

Consent first

Without parental consent recorded by the teacher, the student does not appear on the tablet and the server rejects their assessments.

The voice stays on the tablet

Transcription happens on the device. Audio is only uploaded with explicit consent and is deleted automatically after 90 days.

Minimal data

Only the initial of the student's last name is stored. Each school is isolated from the others at the database level.

Documented legal framework

Impact assessment (DPIA), privacy policy, consent form and processing agreement under Ley 21.719: the school is the controller and Chucao the processor.
