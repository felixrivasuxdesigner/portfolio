Project Overview

AI Reader (LectorIA) is a playful mobile application built for 7-year-old children. It utilizes offline on-device AI speech-to-text to evaluate oral reading accuracy and fluency, matching reading selections to the child's Lexile scale.

The Challenge

Reading out loud in front of teachers, parents, or devices often induces performance anxiety in young readers. Traditional reading software heightens this by displaying aggressive countdown timers and blunt error counters. Furthermore, sending children's audio records to cloud servers raises critical privacy concerns. The challenge was to create an engaging, stress-free, and 100% offline app.

AI Reader Design System

A playful, friendly, and stress-free environment featuring organic rounded corners, soft shadows, and typography optimized for children's reading. Mobile-first, built in React Native with NativeWind.

Value Proposition

We built a child-first reading practice environment centered around on-device voice processing:

  • Offline Whisper.rn Integration: Speech-to-text processing performed locally on the device's chip using lightweight, downloadable AI models (ggml-tiny).
  • Zero-Stress Reading UX: No timers or ticking clocks; the child reads at their own pace accompanied by a friendly owl mascot acting as an empathetic guide.
  • Positive Visual Feedback: Words read correctly are highlighted in green in real-time, and completed pages reward children with collectable stars.
  • Absolute Child Privacy: 100% of the voice audio remains on the device, ensuring native compliance with child protection laws (COPPA/GDPR).

Child-Centered UX Process

Usability Observations

Conducted direct sessions observing children reading aloud to screens, identifying emotional friction points and removing intimidating UI elements.

Mascot Character Design

Designed the friendly owl mascot to provide supportive visual cues, celebrate successes, and offer helpful prompts without generating feelings of failure.

Optimizing Child Typography

Selected the Literata serif font and configured a wide 1.8 line height to improve contrast and help children with early signs of dyslexia scan text easily.

On-Device Whisper Engineering

Compiled native C++ Whisper bindings for React Native, managing dynamic model downloads to keep the initial App Store download package lightweight.
